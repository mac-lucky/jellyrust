# v0.11.0 - JellyRust: Native Jelly with the look of Litefin

JellyRust is a fork of [Native Jelly](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly) 0.10.0 that looks like [Litefin](https://github.com/MoazSalem/litefin), the Jellyfin web client for webOS. It is a separate app, `com.macluckylab.jellyrust`: it installs beside Native Jelly or Litefin and shares no sign-in or data with either. This is its first release.

## What's new

- **The Litefin look.** A pure black theme, the left rail, Litefin's home rows with an immersive hero, its detail pages, player controls, sign-in and settings screens, set in Poppins.
- **Favorites and Random on the rail.** Mark a film, show or person as a favourite with the heart on its page; Random opens a random pick from a library.
- **More on the detail pages:** the box set a film belongs to, the date it was added, its original title and studios, and on a person's page the shows they guest-starred in.
- **Audio and subtitles follow your server.** The app plays the audio and subtitle tracks Jellyfin remembers for you, with **Remember** switches under **Settings > Audio & Subtitles**, and can prefer a dubbed or voice-over (lektor) audio track.
- **Polish**, beside English, Spanish, Russian and Belarusian.
- **No telemetry.** Crash reporting and usage analytics cannot be compiled in, and the app never contacts plex.tv or Plex's metadata service. The only server it talks to is your Jellyfin server.

## Fixed

- **A transcoded film could stop with "Playback failed" after a long pause.** When the server answers the reconnect from the start of the stream, the app now restarts the transcode at the position you paused at.
- **A film left playing when the TV was switched off comes back paused**, and one playing when you switched to another app carries on when you return.
- **Up Next now appears after credits that end in a preview of the next episode**, and leaving during the credits marks the item played.

## Compatibility

Watched by hand on an LG C1 (2021, webOS 6.5.3) against the maintainer's own Jellyfin server, including a DTS film the server transcodes. On every other firmware the app is only known to pass webosbrew's static compatibility check, which this release ran on its package. If you try it on another set, please [report what happened](https://github.com/mac-lucky/jellyrust/issues), whether it worked or not.

## Known issues

- **Never type a password into the app.** It logs keys and typed text to `/tmp` on the TV. Sign in with Quick Connect from another device instead.
- **The long-pause fix has not yet been re-tested on a television** with a real pause into the screen saver.
- **Native Jelly 0.10.0's known issues were not re-checked for this fork.** JellyRust keeps its player and data layer unchanged apart from the fixes above.

## Help test this release

On any LG set other than a C1, sign in with Quick Connect, play a film directly and one your server has to convert, and [tell us what happened](https://github.com/mac-lucky/jellyrust/issues).

## Installing

Download **com.macluckylab.jellyrust_0.11.0_arm.ipk** and install it with the [Homebrew Channel](https://github.com/webosbrew/webos-homebrew-channel) or [dev-manager-desktop](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop). You do **not** need a rooted TV.

Nothing in this distribution chain is signed, so this sha256 is what tells you the file you have is the file published here:

```
__IPK_SHA256__  com.macluckylab.jellyrust_0.11.0_arm.ipk
```
