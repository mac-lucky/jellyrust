## Upstream authors

JellyRust is a modified version of [Native Jelly](https://github.com/sostk/native-jelly) by sostk,
which is a fork of [PlxNative](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native) by Gleb Linnik. Their
copyright notices stay in the code, `LICENSING.md` and `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.

## Design credit

The look follows [Litefin](https://github.com/MoazSalem/litefin) by MoazSalem, a web-based Jellyfin
client for LG TVs. Only the look is borrowed: `docs/litefin-look.md` records measurements and design
decisions taken from Litefin's source and screenshots, and no Litefin code is copied (Litefin is
MPL-2.0). The rail and library icons are Remix Icon glyphs, the same ones Litefin draws, used under
Remix Icon's own Apache-2.0 licence and credited in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.
